About Alan

I'm Appiah-Appiagyei Alan a 15 year old graphic designer and web designer from Ghana. I'm the founder of Alan Studio. I started designing because every small business deserves to look professional and get noticed, even with a small budget.

Because of my age, I see things differently. I grew up online, so I understand what makes people stop scrolling, what looks moden, and how to connect with today's customers. I do most of my design work in Canva, I recently started learning Figma, and I'm about to learn more professional tools like Photoshop and Ilustrator. On the coding side, I'm building my skills in web design and python, and I'm planning to learn app development next.

I'm not just building a portfolio, I'm building partnerships. When you work with me, you get clear comunication, quick turnaround, designs made with your business goals in mind, and affordable rates that make sense for small business. If you're ready to make your business look bigger and more trusted, let's create something great together.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's your typical turnaround time?

Most designs like flyers, posters or logos take 2-3 days. Bigger project like brand identity or websites take a bit longer. I'll give you a clear delivery date before we start, so you know exactly when to expect it.

How many revisions do I get?

Every package comes with 2 free revisions so we can make it perfect. Small tweaks after that are still free, I want you to love the final result.

What file formats will I receive?

You'll get JPG and PNG for online use, PDF for printing, and your editable Canva link so you can make small changes yourself later. If we work in Figma, you'll get that link too.

Do you require upfront payment?

Yes, 50% upfront to secure your spot, and the other 50% once you're happy with the work, before I send the final files. You can pay via MoMo, I'll share the details on your invoice.
